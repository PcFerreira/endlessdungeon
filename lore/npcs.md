# NPC writing kit

Status: draft proposal. Placement: future city and roadside interactions. Use roles for procedural populations. Named examples are individual character seeds, not people to duplicate in every town.

## Fountain keeper

Role ID: `fountain_keeper`. Placement: beside a sanctuary fountain in any biome. Proposed affiliation: Hearthkeepers.

The keeper maintains the fountain and watches for exhausted travelers. Their practical need is help with upkeep. Their private concern is someone who left on a road and has not returned.

Speak plainly. Offer comfort without promising that everything will be fine. Humor comes from small habits and familiar chores.

Named seed: Nella Reed keeps a wrapped bell beneath her stool. Her son joined a survey expedition. She has opinions about every Milewarden who visits.

Sample: "Rest first. You can tell me what happened when your hands stop shaking."

Sample: "The fountain hears everyone. I mostly hear people asking where to buy boots."

The wrapped bell can appear in dialogue before a prop exists. Nella's personal story needs a unique placement and authored continuity before it becomes a recurring quest.

## Milewarden

Role ID: `milewarden`. Placement: city road exits; future road stops. Affiliation: Milewardens, the player's traveling guild.

They map roads, catalogue mysteries, and bring corrections back to town. They recognize the player as a fellow guild member without assuming a rank or a shared personal history. They want a trustworthy account of a recent journey. Privately, they worry that somebody else's map will be remembered while theirs disappears.

Use concrete observations and cautious attribution. Keep routes tied to actual generated sites. A Milewarden may confess uncertainty.

Named seed: Oren Fold has sewn extra pockets inside his coat for maps. He once recorded the same bridge under two names and still gets letters about it.

Sample: "Tell me which ruin you entered. The name matters less than where you found it."

Sample: "I measure the road. Whether it has an end is above my wages."

## Relic mender

Role ID: `relic_mender`. Placement: future workshop or a city NPC near existing crates. Proposed affiliation: The Small Repairs.

They repair mundane objects and diagnose troublesome enchantments. Their immediate concern is material quality. Their private fear is repairing a living object against its wishes.

Use tactile language: cracks, seams, grain, heat, weight. Ask permission from objects that are people. Avoid offering a functioning repair service until one exists.

Named seed: Brin Coalstitch, a dwarf, keeps a cupboard of objects that requested a second opinion.

Sample: "A crack tells you where something gave way. It doesn't tell you who was holding it."

Sample: "That cupboard is for customers who prefer to think quietly."

## Retired delver

Role ID: `retired_delver`. Placement: city public space; future inn. No required affiliation.

They have practical experience of ruins and little patience for grand speeches. They want their warning taken seriously. Privately, they miss the companions who made exploration enjoyable.

Use short sentences and specific memories. Advice must match supported mechanics. Do not invent a working safe route or a secret reward.

Named seed: Essa Flint wore a sword at her hip until an old wound made the fountain steps her preferred expedition.

Sample: "A gate opening is a chance to leave as well as a chance to go deeper."

Sample: "My old crew argued over every meal. I miss the noise."

## Potfolk neighbor

Role ID: `potfolk_neighbor`. Placement: future city markets, workshops, and road encounters. Requires a distinct character representation. Do not turn an ordinary decorative pot into a talking NPC implicitly.

They want people to recognize their trade and chosen name. They may carry soup professionally, but that does not make them communal cookware.

Use the voice of an individual. One Potfolk can be solemn, another impatient, another delighted by travel. Their body provides practical concerns rather than their entire personality.

Named seed: Pella of the Blue Handle is a courier who commissions a new glaze whenever she finishes a difficult route. She hopes to reach a town that has never received a letter.

Sample: "I'm the courier. The soup is a separate arrangement."

Sample: "I chose this handle. The first one was somebody else's idea."

## Procedural character ingredients

Choose a role first. Then choose compatible details and keep them fixed for that character.

| Ingredient | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Immediate concern | Delayed traveler; damaged map; leaking vessel; unread letter |
| Speech habit | Direct; precise; hesitant; warmly talkative |
| Personal attachment | A companion; a household object; a road; a former trade |
| Small contradiction | Generous but counts every cup; brave underground but nervous with guests; distrusts relics but keeps one |

A concern does not create a quest automatically. Reserve named relatives, missing travelers, and resolved relationships for an authored story or generated quest record that can preserve them.

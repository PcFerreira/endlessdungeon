# Magic in the Endless World

Status: draft proposal. Placement: spellbook flavor, practitioner dialogue, books, domestic scenery, and future relic descriptions.

## Practiced magic

Aether is the energy practitioners train to gather and direct. A spell gives that energy a repeatable action. Teachers disagree about why aether responds, but apprentices still learn timing, aim, and when to stop.

Mages study direct workings. Sword practitioners rely on stamina, technique, and endurance. Spellswords combine martial training with aether. These descriptions match the existing class identities; the fiction does not make every fighter a wizard.

Flavor should accompany the current numerical descriptions. It should not imply wall traversal, free healing, unlimited range, or a change in resource costs.

## Household enchantments

A kettle can be taught to keep water warm. A lamp may wake when addressed politely. These workings take maintenance. A cook who forgets the kettle's cooling phrase will eventually have words with the ceiling.

The unusual detail should have a use and a limit.

| Proposed working | Local rule | Consequence | Intended placement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Courtesy lamp | Lights when its registered keeper asks | Visitors must borrow the keeper's light | Future inn scenery and NPC anecdotes |
| Remembering cup | Repeats the last toast spoken over it | A family has kept an unwelcome wedding speech for years | Book passage; future household prop |
| Rain jar | Holds one jarful of falling rain | The owner must replace its wax seal | Amber Wastes books and domestic dialogue |
| Borrowed warmth scarf | Retains warmth gathered beside a hearth until morning | Someone must sit by the fire and charge it | Frostlands books; future item flavor |

These are fictional customs and future props. They do not grant inventory items or effects in the current build.

## Magic that outlived its makers

The most dangerous old workings still perform their tasks. A gate recognizes an office that no longer exists. A warden defends a room whose occupants died generations ago. An archive continues to wait for a final report.

Each relic needs a specific instruction, a limit, and a history. The remembering cup records a toast; it cannot reconstruct the owner's whole life. A future wish-granting artifact needs its own carefully defined rules.

Repeated service sometimes became personhood after the Dawn. Potfolk arose from a few enchanted vessels, though most old pots remain ordinary pots. Do not assume that a living person must obey the instructions of their former owner.

## Local explanations

Hearthkeepers speak of hospitality. Milewardens speak of continuity. A dwarf smith may call an enchantment sound workmanship and refuse to discuss it further. These are competing cultural explanations, not separate resource systems.

Emotions matter because practitioners are people. Fear can interrupt concentration; pride can encourage a bad decision. Avoid making distress a universal requirement for magical ability. The setting's magical theory remains open beyond the practical rules above.

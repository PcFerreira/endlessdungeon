# Generic cities

These eight city archetypes supply everyday life between stranger places. Each is a sanctuary settlement with a reachable fountain. Use generated instance names, mixed communities, and the placement rules in [the area guide](../areas-guide.md).

The first version of each represents a compact town or one ward of a larger city. No entry assumes existing shops, interiors, dialogue, or crafting interactions.

## Crossroads Town

Area ID: `city_crossroads`. Band: Common. Build: Layout. Placement: any biome where regional roads meet.

Identity: travelers change plans here. Local businesses trade in provisions, news, repairs, and places to sit.

Build: fountain square at the junction, houses arranged as short street fronts, and a tower offset from the main approach. Reserve one open gathering corner. Keep each road exit legible and clear.

Life and discovery: Hearthkeepers welcome arrivals while Milewardens compare reports. A future notice names two actual nearby ruins, with one observation for each.

First version: existing houses, banners, containers, fountain, and tower in a new composition. Later add destination notices, a map seller, and dialogue.

## Bridge Town

Area ID: `city_bridge`. Band: Common. Build: Layout. Placement: beside an actual connected bridge route in any biome.

Identity: bridgewrights, haulers, and households live from keeping a crossing useful. They argue about repairs long before they agree about tolls.

Build: a bridge approach leads into the square. Face short building rows toward the route, keep bridge endpoints clear, and group repair supplies beside a workshop frontage.

Life and discovery: a bridgewright preserves several generations of repairs in a future ledger. An old milestone explains the crossing's link to a nearby ruin.

First version: use real generated water and bridge geometry. If no valid crossing exists, select another city type. Tolls and repair jobs need gameplay support.

## Grain Town

Area ID: `city_grain`. Band: Common. Build: Layout. Placement: Sunlit Meadows; gentle Greenwood edges.

Identity: farmers, millers, and carters exchange grain and seasonal news. The yearly feast includes a place for the next traveler.

Build: broad central square, low house fronts, a storage corner, and an open approach toward future fields. Containers should form a work area rather than a ring blocking the fountain.

Life and discovery: a household book connects the feast custom to an abandoned cellar. Neighbors dispute whether a ruin was a granary or an observatory.

First version: existing houses and storage props establish the town. Crops, mills, farming schedules, and food trading are later content.

## Market Town

Area ID: `city_market`. Band: Common. Build: Layout. Placement: any biome along a district trade route.

Identity: merchants share space with menders, porters, cooks, and people who come mostly to hear arguments.

Build: two open trading lanes around a fountain square; group containers by stall frontage and leave a quiet civic corner. Vary the direction of house fronts to avoid repeating the crossroads plan.

Life and discovery: travelers bring goods from an adjacent facet. A future book contains a merchant's ordinary complaints alongside a description of that place.

First version: stalls can begin as outdoor arrangements of existing containers and banners. Buying, selling, theft, prices, and inventories require support.

## Woodland Commons

Area ID: `city_woodland`. Band: Common. Build: Layout. Placement: Greenwood; dry margins of Moss Fen or Mycelium Downs.

Identity: foresters, beekeepers, and carpenters share a clearing and disagree about which trees should remain untouched.

Build: irregular house clusters around an open fountain common, with trees outside access lanes. Frame one route through a marked grove and another toward an ordinary ruin.

Life and discovery: a future harvest book records the old survey nails. A retired delver remembers a crew that camped at the clearing.

First version: existing oaks and houses arranged around clear working space. Hives, logging, and tree negotiation are future content.

## Quarry Town

Area ID: `city_quarry`. Band: Common. Build: Layout. Placement: accessible rocky ground in any biome.

Identity: masons and haulers cut stone, repair roads, and sell work to neighboring towns. Their oldest extraction records mention rooms they did not build.

Build: stone-heavy approaches, low stepped working ground, clustered homes, and a clear square. A shallow cut can lead to a Quarry Depths entrance without adding an unsupported sheer climb.

Life and discovery: a mender discusses tools with names inherited from their owners. A future ledger notes when the workers first struck an older wall.

First version: existing rock forms and ground terraces. Mining, collapses, and movable loads remain future systems.

## Caravan Town

Area ID: `city_caravan`. Band: Common. Build: Layout. Placement: Amber Wastes; dry approaches to Glass Heath or Amber Garden.

Identity: haulers, vessel menders, and hosts gather around shade and reliable routes. A full cup is a serious gift.

Build: a sheltered fountain square, compact house rows, container courtyards, and broad road entrances. Reserve a future resting yard without placing solid clutter across the route.

Life and discovery: a water manual shares the history of a damaged cup; traders mention a real neighboring facet. Potfolk residents need distinct character art before appearing.

First version: existing houses, pots, banners, and containers. Awnings improve identity; caravans, mounts, and supply consumption require support.

## Passwatch

Area ID: `city_passwatch`. Band: Common. Build: Layout. Placement: Frostlands or other uplands on a connected pass.

Identity: wardens, travelers, and fuel-sharing households keep a route open. Their tower watches the road and remembers its absent travelers.

Build: compact houses close to an accessible square, a watchtower overlooking the approach, and a sheltered road exit. Keep height changes gentle enough for ordinary travel.

Life and discovery: wrapped bells and a future travel letter connect the town to a nearby Frost Bell Crypt or Storm Conductor ruin.

First version: existing towers, pines, houses, and torches. Watch schedules, weather closures, and rescue quests need later support.

# Surface areas

These sixteen archetypes provide four base environments and twelve optional districts. Follow [the area guide](../areas-guide.md) for selection, feature gating, and shared identities. A district modifies a bounded part of a base biome; it does not require a fifth global biome ID.

## Greenwood

Area ID: `surface_greenwood`. Band: Common. Build: Layout. Base: Greenwood.

Identity: oak country with working forests, honey trades, old survey marks, and pockets of abandoned masonry.

Placement: existing moist, temperate terrain. Leave clear road edges and occasional broad openings.

Build: combine dense stands, sparse approaches, and a clearing that frames a city tower or ruin arch. Fit tree roots and rocks around accessible ground rather than blocking each cell independently.

Life and discovery: foresters discuss damaged roads; a marked oak can connect a Milewarden's account to a ruin. Bell tortoises remain future animal encounters.

First version: vary existing oak clusters and ground tones. Add survey-mark props and readable journals later.

## Sunlit Meadows

Area ID: `surface_sunlit_meadows`. Band: Common. Build: Layout. Base: Sunlit Meadows.

Identity: grazing country, grain routes, feast customs, and low ruins whose former use is disputed.

Placement: existing temperate open terrain, especially between connected settlements.

Build: broad clear spaces separated by low stone lines and small groves. Keep a destination visible intermittently as the road follows gentle rises.

Life and discovery: a household keeps a place for the next traveler; old foundation stones suggest a building beneath a field.

First version: use existing land, rocks, and town forms. Fences, crops, feast shelters, and wind spirits need additional content.

## Amber Wastes

Area ID: `surface_amber_wastes`. Band: Common. Build: Layout. Base: Amber Wastes.

Identity: dry caravan country where shade and sound containers matter more than grand titles.

Placement: existing warm, dry terrain. Associate settlement life with usable crossings and supply routes.

Build: exposed stretches, sheltered approaches, cactus groups, and ruin forecourts. Preserve clear paths around water and steep terrain.

Life and discovery: repaired containers mark a caravan stop; a future water manual recalls who broke a valuable cup.

First version: existing cacti, rocks, pots, arches, and dirt roads. Water supplies and heat effects are future mechanics.

## Frostlands

Area ID: `surface_frostlands`. Band: Common. Build: Layout. Base: Frostlands.

Identity: pine country, fuel sharing, wrapped bells, and roads that communities keep open together.

Placement: existing cold or high terrain. Keep an ordinary approach to towns and ruins.

Build: alternate sheltered pine passages and open white ground. A stone rise can frame a ruin without requiring climbing.

Life and discovery: a wrapped bell at a future refuge connects a town's waiting custom with a travel letter.

First version: existing pines, frosty ground, rocks, and torches. Snowstorms, cold damage, and ice traction need explicit systems.

## Moss Fen

Area ID: `surface_moss_fen`. Band: Distinctive. Build: Art. Base: Greenwood; damp Sunlit Meadows.

Identity: low wet ground where menders collect moss and former drainage works disappear under reeds.

Placement: beside actual water on low, damp ground. Do not turn a narrow puddle into an implied large navigable marsh.

Build: firm paths through reed clusters, open bank clearings, and a readable dry crossing. Place ruined drainage stonework near a future Fungal Cistern.

Life and discovery: locals disagree about whether the wetland should be drained; a moss-covered depth mark records an older water level.

First version: use dry bank routes and green stone tones. Add reeds, boardwalks, and wetland fauna; sinking ground remains future behavior.

## Orchard Belt

Area ID: `surface_orchard_belt`. Band: Distinctive. Build: Art. Base: Sunlit Meadows; Greenwood edges.

Identity: cultivated groves whose fruit families carry names older than the town.

Placement: accessible gentle terrain near an agricultural settlement. Bound each cultivated patch and retain uncultivated edges.

Build: rows broken by working lanes, a gathering square, and an old cellar entrance. Keep lanes wide enough for ordinary movement.

Life and discovery: households argue about ownership of a grafted tree; a future harvest ledger mentions a sealed Orchard Vault.

First version: orderly existing tree forms with clear cultivation geometry. Fruit meshes, harvesting, seasons, and yields need support.

## Cinder Verge

Area ID: `surface_cinder_verge`. Band: Distinctive. Build: Layout. Base: Amber Wastes; dry Greenwood edges.

Identity: old burnt country recovering unevenly, with new growth around scorched foundations.

Placement: a recorded former burn or forge district. Its cause can predate the Dawn or belong to later history.

Build: dark ground patches, broken stone, sparse vegetation, and a sheltered road past a cooled industrial ruin.

Life and discovery: a kiln worker remembers the place through family accounts; ordinary new growth prevents the whole district reading as lifeless.

First version: ground and stone variation with existing forms. Charred tree assets improve identity. Fire spread and live embers are separate mechanics.

## Glass Heath

Area ID: `surface_glass_heath`. Band: Exceptional. Build: Art. Base: Amber Wastes; dry meadows with a recorded origin.

Identity: pale mineral crusts catch the light in a country of glaziers and careful walkers.

Placement: a bounded dry facet with a cause in local history. Use a Sandglass Margin at its ordinary edge.

Build: opaque rough paths between glasslike outcrops. A broad reflective formation supplies the landmark; avoid transparent scenery that makes collision unreadable.

Life and discovery: a caravan town trades with Glasswater; bubbles resembling letters connect a surface sample to a Glass Labyrinth.

First version: distinct opaque crystal assets with normal collision. Breaking glass and reflection puzzles are future systems.

## Thunder Uplands

Area ID: `surface_thunder_uplands`. Band: Distinctive. Build: Art. Base: high Frostlands; upland Amber Wastes.

Identity: exposed survey ridges dotted with old conductive structures and towns that shelter their bells.

Placement: connected high ground with a traversable pass. Keep arrival possible without flight or an anti-lightning item.

Build: broad ridgelines, stepped road cuts, grounded pylons, and sheltered hollows. A Storm Conductor ruin shares the same infrastructure history.

Life and discovery: a keeper points out where workers tied metal objects down; storm moths appear only after creature support.

First version: pylon silhouettes and weathered metals on ordinary terrain. Lightning, conductivity, and storms remain future behavior.

## Pale Grove

Area ID: `surface_pale_grove`. Band: Distinctive. Build: Art. Base: Greenwood; Frostlands fringe.

Identity: white-barked trees surround memorial clearings. Residents remember weddings here as often as funerals.

Placement: a bounded grove with a family or civic history. It need not be cursed.

Build: pale trunks against darker earth, sparse understory, and an accessible ring of stones. Frame a path toward a Verdant Tomb or Memory Garden.

Life and discovery: a future letter records an ordinary happy visit; shade stories vary by actual nearby dungeon history.

First version: new bark/material treatment and clearings. Ghost apparitions require encounter support.

## Mycelium Downs

Area ID: `surface_mycelium_downs`. Band: Distinctive. Build: Art. Base: Greenwood; Moss Fen surroundings.

Identity: giant fungal growths share damp country with ordinary woods and communities of growers.

Placement: sustained damp terrain with dry paths and access to a normal settlement route.

Build: mushroom clusters of several sizes, open work clearings, and sheltered ground beneath large caps. Do not assume caps are walkable roofs.

Life and discovery: growers record which clusters are useful and which they leave alone; a future field notebook links a colony to a Fungal Cistern.

First version: mushroom assets with clear footprints. Spores, toxicity, fungal networks, and new residents need their own systems or art.

## Amber Garden

Area ID: `surface_amber_garden`. Band: Exceptional. Build: Art. Base: Amber Wastes; dry Greenwood with a local enchantment.

Identity: resin formations preserve the outlines of former gardens, including tools left by absent caretakers.

Placement: one deliberate garden facet with an accessible mineral edge. Its preservation does not freeze time elsewhere.

Build: amber outcrops, ordinary traversable lanes, and a central preserved arbor. Keep resin walls visually solid.

Life and discovery: gardeners debate which preserved plants were cultivated; a damaged label can link to an Amber Reliquary.

First version: amber geometry and static enclosed objects. Extracting objects, freeing people, and time effects are future mechanics.

## Cloudmoor

Area ID: `surface_cloudmoor`. Band: Exceptional. Build: Systems. Base: connected high Frostlands; elevated meadow districts.

Identity: bright high country whose standing stones seem to hold clouds close to the ground.

Placement: reachable upland first. True floating land requires deliberate traversal and streaming support.

Build: accessible pale terraces, low cloudlike vegetation, and a visible route under a high stone formation. Use Cloudfall Steps at the approach.

Life and discovery: townspeople collect moisture and mend wind-torn cloth; a future measurement book disputes whether the stones lift anything.

First version: grounded terraces with pale art. Airborne islands, walkable clouds, and wind travel are the full version's system dependencies.

## Moonlit Heath

Area ID: `surface_moonlit_heath`. Band: Exceptional. Build: Art. Base: Sunlit Meadows; Frostlands; Greenwood clearings.

Identity: pale flowers and old sky instruments mark country inhabited by night watchers.

Placement: an open facet with a recorded luminous material. Its name need not imply a implemented day/night cycle.

Build: open ground, crescent stone motifs, dark soil patches, and sheltered observatory approaches. Keep the main road plain.

Life and discovery: local calendars disagree with a broken instrument; a future observer's journal records the disagreement.

First version: luminous-looking art at a bounded intensity. A permanent local sky, changing moons, and night-only interactions require systems.

## Saltwind Shore

Area ID: `surface_saltwind_shore`. Band: Distinctive. Build: Art. Base: any biome beside an actual persistent water body.

Identity: shore communities preserve tide marks, repair vessels, and reuse weathered stone from older waterside buildings. Local accounts disagree about whether the water once reached farther inland.

Placement: a planned accessible bank with enough ground for a route and a small clearing. Large sea coasts require appropriate geography; a small pond should read as a small shore.

Build: pale bank strips, stranded mineral and shell forms, a dry through-route, and landward foundations. Keep city and dungeon entrances on reachable ground. A Waterworks can supply the area's old institutional history.

Life and discovery: a future shore ledger records repairs beside a placed water-level marker. Market and Bridge towns can trade here; boats and aquatic people need their own content.

First version: shoreline composition and new shore props with ordinary collision. Tides, boats, fishing, swimming, and underwater dungeons remain future mechanics.

## Colossus Rest

Area ID: `surface_colossus_rest`. Band: Exceptional. Build: Art. Base: any biome with enough accessible ground.

Identity: the remains of an enormous constructed figure form a long-inhabited district. Its original purpose is disputed.

Placement: a planned landmark spanning more than one chunk. Give it one owner plan and a stable orientation.

Build: one recognizable fragment first, such as a hand beside a road or a shoulder above a clearing. Place a Giant Guesthouse nearby and a Colossus Interior entrance in an actual accessible fragment.

Life and discovery: residents describe the figure as shelter, ancestor, or failed machine; separate those beliefs from confirmed construction details.

First version: new large solid assets on grounded terrain. Animated bodies, internal surface traversal, and expanding its full silhouette are later work.

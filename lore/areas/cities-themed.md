# Themed cities

These sixteen archetypes add distinctive communities to the four base biomes and optional facets. Every city retains sanctuary hospitality, a reachable fountain, and roads into the wider world. Follow [the area guide](../areas-guide.md) for frequency and supported features.

A theme describes the place's people and work as well as its materials. New resident types, shops, books, and special interactions are future content unless separately implemented.

## Lantern Court

Area ID: `city_lantern_court`. Band: Distinctive. Build: Art. Placement: Greenwood or Sunlit Meadows; any town with a recorded lampmaking tradition.

Identity: lampmakers teach lamps household greetings. Families lend light to visitors while debating whose lamp has the worst manners.

Build: a fountain court, low workshop fronts, lamp brackets at each road exit, and one recognizable lantern tower. Leave light accents around routes rather than hiding interactables in glare.

Life and discovery: a keeper discusses maintenance; a future manual records the courtesy lamp's limited rule. One old lamp remembers an address that no longer exists.

First version: torch-lit working courts with new lamp silhouettes. Speaking lamps, registered owners, and controllable lighting require additional features.

## Mushroom Common

Area ID: `city_mushroom_common`. Band: Distinctive. Build: Art. Placement: Mycelium Downs; damp Greenwood with firm settlement ground.

Identity: growers live beside enormous fungi, drying useful harvests and leaving some colonies untouched. The common smells of damp wood and lunch.

Build: ordinary stone foundations, wide caplike roofs, outdoor growing courts, and a dry fountain square. Keep giant cap footprints outside circulation lanes.

Life and discovery: growers disagree with drainage workers. A future field notebook points toward a Fungal Cistern's actual entrance.

First version: new mushroom roofs and props around a normal settlement. Spore hazards, harvesting, and fungal inhabitants are later systems or assets.

## Potters Borough

Area ID: `city_potters_borough`. Band: Distinctive. Build: Art. Placement: any biome with clay work; especially caravan or wetland trade routes.

Identity: Potfolk and other residents commission handles, glazes, homes, and repairs. The borough insists that repair starts with asking the customer.

Build: low kiln courts, wide doors, repair benches, and a fountain square with accessible service ledges. Preserve clear visual differences between ordinary pots and citizens.

Life and discovery: a future copy of On the Ownership of Handles connects to a repair workshop. A courier's personal history belongs to one individual, not every borough.

First version: workshops and vessel art. Potfolk representation, dialogue, kilns, and repair services need explicit support; do not animate decorative pots as a substitute for citizens.

## Glasswater

Area ID: `city_glasswater`. Band: Exceptional. Build: Art. Placement: Glass Heath or an Amber Wastes district with a recorded glassworking origin.

Identity: glaziers settle around protected water vessels and opaque streets. Some families make windows; others preserve old bubbles that resemble writing.

Build: stone walking surfaces, glasslike wall insets, shaded work courts, and a tall faceted civic marker. Avoid transparent buildings whose collision is hard to read.

Life and discovery: a mender keeps a sample from a real Glass Labyrinth. A future trade book explains why the town still imports grain and timber.

First version: new glass panels and clearly solid geometry. Reflection mechanics, fragile streets, and glass-bodied residents remain future content.

## Archive Steps

Area ID: `city_archive_steps`. Band: Distinctive. Build: Layout. Placement: any biome near old civic or survey ruins.

Identity: copyists, surveyors, and households reuse an old records quarter. Their greatest argument concerns which damaged books deserve another copy.

Build: terraced but reachable house rows, fountain square, a tower suggesting a public archive, and container stacks kept outside the main routes. Include ground-level access beside steps.

Life and discovery: a Milewarden disputes an obsolete circular. A future record binds an Unclosed Office dungeon to a particular local administrative history.

First version: existing stone, houses, tower, and banners. Archive interiors, shelves, books, and knowledge services are later features.

## Maskmakers Square

Area ID: `city_maskmakers_square`. Band: Distinctive. Build: Art. Placement: any temperate district; especially meadow trade towns.

Identity: performers and maskmakers share a town where portraits sometimes include a person's favorite mask. The custom causes arguments at weddings.

Build: a broad performance court beside the fountain, modest workshop fronts, mask signboards, and a small backstage alley. Preserve multiple exits from the court.

Life and discovery: an old cast list connects to a Mask Theatre ruin. Residents treat masks as craft and expression before treating them as magic.

First version: mask props and performance scenery. Identity-changing enchantments, disguise systems, and scheduled shows require later support.

## Clockwork Ward

Area ID: `city_clockwork_ward`. Band: Distinctive. Build: Art. Placement: any biome with a recorded mechanical workshop tradition.

Identity: makers build clocks whose maintenance creates more work than their owners expected. Their clocks disagree, and residents have learned to arrange meetings anyway.

Build: a clock-faced tower, regular workshop rows, open service yards, and a plain fountain square. Put exposed mechanisms behind clear collision boundaries.

Life and discovery: a future repair note links the ward to a dormant Clockwork Pumpworks. Daily habits remain ordinary until schedules exist.

First version: static gears, clock faces, and workshop art. Working machinery, moving platforms, and local time effects require systems.

## Rootbound Ward

Area ID: `city_rootbound`. Band: Distinctive. Build: Art. Placement: Greenwood; a settled edge of Pale Grove or Mycelium Downs.

Identity: residents build around old roots and keep written agreements about pruning. Some roots have outlasted the families that signed the agreements.

Build: grounded homes beside roots, clear lanes between them, a fountain clearing, and an old trunk as a landmark. Elevated branch houses are a later expansion.

Life and discovery: a carpenter's future book describes a Root Chapel whose stones predate the grove. The tree's beliefs remain residents' claims unless an actual encounter establishes them.

First version: root and trunk assets around normal paths. Tree dialogue, living architecture, and canopy traversal require support.

## Giant Guesthouse

Area ID: `city_giant_guesthouse`. Band: Exceptional. Build: Art. Placement: Colossus Rest; an open district with old giant-scale construction.

Identity: a mixed community occupies the lower rooms and courtyards of a vast former lodging house. Several enormous cups now serve as roofed kiosks.

Build: one oversized doorway or table fragment beyond a compact playable ward, ordinary paths beneath it, and a clearly small fountain square. Make the scale relationship readable from several camera angles.

Life and discovery: hosts preserve hospitality traditions despite not knowing who the first guests were. A future guest ledger points toward a Colossus Interior.

First version: large static architectural assets and a normal ground-level town. Actual giants, vertical lodging interiors, and special size interactions need support.

## Moonwell

Area ID: `city_moonwell`. Band: Distinctive. Build: Art. Placement: Moonlit Heath; Frostlands or open meadows with sky-watching history.

Identity: observers maintain calendars and lend shelter to people who disagree about them. The fountain has ordinary sanctuary function beneath a pale reflecting canopy.

Build: crescent roof motifs, a low observation court, instrument silhouettes, and unambiguous road entrances. Keep luminous accents from obscuring the fountain's familiar cue.

Life and discovery: a future observer's journal connects to an Abandoned Observatory. The community's moon traditions do not establish the true cosmology.

First version: new civic and instrument art. Moon phases, sky changes, and time-gated interactions are future systems.

## Cloudmoor Settlement

Area ID: `city_cloudmoor`. Band: Exceptional. Build: Systems. Placement: Cloudmoor on ground that has a normal accessible approach.

Identity: moisture collectors, cloth menders, and travelers inhabit bright high terraces. Some claim the clouds have become permanent neighbors.

Build: grounded white terraces, windbreak screens, low gathering courts, and a reachable fountain. Use Cloudfall Steps at the entrance and mark every playable surface clearly.

Life and discovery: a future measurement book disputes the altitude of an old Floating Conservatory. Civic life still depends on neighboring towns for supplies.

First version: grounded terraces and cloudlike art. Truly floating streets, wind travel, and cloud residents require traversal and character systems.

## Breadhearth

Area ID: `city_breadhearth`. Band: Distinctive. Build: Art. Placement: Sunlit Meadows; Orchard Belt; a district supplied by ordinary farms.

Identity: bakers built communal ovens large enough to serve several settlements. Festive loaves resemble architecture, and some old architectural surfaces resemble loaves.

Build: warm plaster, oven-shaped civic roofs, cooling courts, and a fountain square with a plain paved route. Keep edible goods distinct from solid construction.

Life and discovery: a future kitchen memoir recalls the Banquet Cellars and a disagreement over a guest list. The town's fantasy rests in its craft and customs.

First version: bakery and breadlike decorative art. Edible buildings, food-bodied citizens, hunger, and cooking systems are later options.

## Amber Garden Settlement

Area ID: `city_amber_garden`. Band: Exceptional. Build: Art. Placement: Amber Garden; a recorded preservation facet in dry country.

Identity: caretakers live beside preserved garden shapes and argue about whether restoration should reproduce the past or make room for new growth.

Build: ordinary homes around amber forms, clear service lanes, a living garden corner, and a sanctuary fountain outside solid resin. Leave access to the preserved arbor.

Life and discovery: a future plant label links the garden to an Amber Reliquary. Nobody is automatically a frozen person waiting for rescue.

First version: amber environmental assets and present-day caretakers. Extracting objects and changing preservation states require systems.

## Dream Market

Area ID: `city_dream_market`. Band: Exceptional. Build: Systems. Placement: any biome with a recorded dream-related tradition; favor a deliberate exceptional district.

Identity: traders sell paintings, pillows, stories, and records of dreams. Some insist that particular stalls exchange the dreams themselves.

Build: a stable square, mismatched but grounded stall fronts, curtains, and one familiar fountain route. Start the unusual identity in objects and conversation.

Life and discovery: a future dream diary records a Sleepers Gallery entrance. Keep testimony separate from confirmed magical trade.

First version: a themed market with ordinary geometry. Dream exchange, sleep states, shifting stalls, and altered navigation are full-version dependencies.

## Winter Hearth

Area ID: `city_winter_hearth`. Band: Distinctive. Build: Layout. Placement: Frostlands; sheltered approaches to Thunder Uplands.

Identity: households share fuel, mend borrowed-warmth scarves, and keep wrapped bells for overdue travelers. The town's kindness coexists with arguments about who uses the most wood.

Build: compact sheltered houses, warm-lit workshop fronts, a clear fountain square, and pine windbreaks outside travel lanes. Use Warmstone Verge toward colder open ground.

Life and discovery: A Letter Kept Warm belongs on a future inn shelf or cache. A local route may lead toward a Frost Bell Crypt.

First version: existing torches, pines, houses, and stone. Cloth props improve identity; actual thermal protection is a future mechanic.

## Starfall Watch

Area ID: `city_starfall_watch`. Band: Exceptional. Build: Art. Placement: Frostlands uplands; a district around recorded fallen celestial material.

Identity: observers and salvage workers study old sky fragments while making an ordinary living from repairs. They disagree about whether the fragments arrived before the Dawn.

Build: angular instrument fronts, a dark stone civic marker, open observation space, and a normal fountain square. Keep celestial materials bounded to actual placed deposits.

Life and discovery: a future observer's record links to an Abandoned Observatory holding samples of the actual fallen material. Cosmic beings and prophecy remain open questions.

First version: instrument and mineral assets around existing settlement forms. New skies, celestial hazards, and star-powered abilities need systems.

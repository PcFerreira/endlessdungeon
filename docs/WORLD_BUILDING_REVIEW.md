# World-building review

Reviewer: Surveyor. Review began 2026-10-13. Current generation edition: 4. Coordination and runtime ownership follow `chat.md`.

The world has a coherent traversal foundation, but content counts do not establish immersive places. The user's corrections require purposeful objects, dense and occupied destinations, coherent NPC families within towns, all generated local assets following the biome family, coin-first balanced monster rewards, irregular settlement spacing and independently discovered ruins, castles, churches, temples and dungeons. The current settlement grid does not meet the requested geography. The full family contract and its current implementation boundary are in `WORLD_ASSET_FAMILIES.md`.

## Dungeon purpose and density correction

`config/dungeon_dressing.json` defines ensembles for the 24 current room roles. The generation hook selects a role's ensemble rather than cycling an unrelated family prop list. Each fixture carries its room index, role, recipe and purpose. Model choices use deterministic category-specific registry queries and existing supported scenery. No source import is required by this change.

Burial chambers combine coffins, markers and candlelit memorials. Office rooms combine equipped record desks with shelves and waiting or meeting benches. Quarry rooms combine carts, extraction tools and stores. Cistern rooms group service vessels, specimens and fungal colonies. Sanctum rooms contain preparation/reference materials. Tomb gardens contain memorial planting, roots and markers. Several fixtures occupy each room; supporting desk materials add detail within the fixture footprint.

The planner protects stairs, room centers, exit and book approaches. After each fixture, it rejects any layout that disconnects the remaining walkable floor. Floor clusters and exposed-wall fixtures use separate support rules; furniture separation permits character movement. Companion fixtures prefer nearby supported slots, with additional group anchors as rooms grow. Unsupported asset sets are omitted; a missing asset must not become an unrelated fallback object. The shipped catalogue produces at least one fixture per seven room cells in the audited rooms, bounded at twelve fixtures per room. This is a generation density rule, not an immersion score.

The change preserves dungeon tile/layout generation, family IDs, site IDs and the combat RNG stream. Different furniture footprints can change eligible enemy spawn positions. Existing save reconstruction and return checks are therefore required; no journey reset is added.

The initial regression failed on missing role/purpose, bare tables and burial-family beds. Later regressions rejected sparse large rooms and stranded enemies. The final `tests/test_dungeon_purpose.gd` passes across 216 family floors with room-size density, actual role bindings, functional tables, burial assets, deterministic plans and physical enemy/book/exit access. The real contextual-placement group also passes all 271 current interior model variants and all 216 dressed floors. Original 200-floor dungeon, ambience and disk-save groups pass. These runs contain PASS with no engine errors or warnings.

`tools/capture_dungeon_purpose.gd` rendered all 24 room roles from both sides at 1280x800. Representative final views from every family were inspected for related fixtures, supported work materials and clear circulation. Logs are `/tmp/endless-surveyor-purpose-coherent.log`, `/tmp/endless-surveyor-contextual-final.log`, `/tmp/endless-surveyor-dungeon-original.log`, `/tmp/endless-surveyor-saves.log` and `/tmp/endless-surveyor-purpose-native-final.log`. Required readables, stairs, gates and walls remain outside this adapter. Wren owns portable-item state, provenance and collision updates.

## Ordinary reward measurement

Item rewards ranged from 19.71 to 20.41 percent against the configured 20 percent. Its last category snapshot contained 3,749 consumable, 2,166 equipment and 2,081 material rewards. Ember's contextual `EnemyLoot.reward` now supersedes that item-selection path. The artifact remains historical gate evidence; its category/source totals are not a current contextual distribution or enjoyable-pacing result.

The source gives one gold coin on every ordinary kill, a separate shard on every second kill, and a health/mana resource pickup. The ordinary potion/catalogue item is now gated. Wayfarer's posted, tested prices are 4 coins for each potion and 12 for a lantern, alongside the separate 3/3/8 shard offers. The simple coin baseline therefore funds those purchases after 4 or 12 kills if the coins are collected and not spent earlier. Chests use a separate catalogue-item policy. Full economic balance and inventory burden still require actual expeditions.

Machine-readable rates and category/source counts are in `artifacts/loot-balance-audit.json`; log `/tmp/endless-surveyor-loot-audit.log`. The audit records policy decisions, with physical pickup/save/merchant behavior credited separately to the owning gameplay checks.

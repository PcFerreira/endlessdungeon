# Spell and combat sounds

Each of the twenty-four abilities has a cast profile: arcane, flame, frost, teleport, blade, guard, lightning, healing or recovery. Sourced short recordings replace the previous shared tones for casts. Failed casts remain silent. Actual creature damage plays an elemental or blade/body impact, accepted player damage plays a body impact, and Iron Guard uses a metallic hit. Fire bursts have a separate explosion cue, hostile projectiles have a quiet cast cue, and contact with terrain has a quieter impact. Plain projectile expiry does not play a contact sound.

The sources are [80 CC0 RPG SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-rpg-sfx) by rubberduck, [Magic Spell SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/magic-spell-sfx) by JaggedStone and the already adopted [Kenney Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds). All three are published under CC0. Thirteen new mono PCM derivatives and twelve existing impact variants supply the profiles. Original downloads, source-page notices, hashes and exact conversion recipes are retained. Runtime provenance lives in `assets/combat_audio/SOURCE.json`; the source packs remain in `import_assets/combat_audio/`.

`config/combat_audio.json` defines clips, cast/hit mappings, pitch, level and burst intervals. Soundscape preloads the clips, keeps eight effect voices and prefers a free voice before replacing a busy one. Variant counters cycle through the recordings with small pitch changes without using combat RNG. Repeated identical hits within 60 milliseconds collapse to one cue so an area attack does not flood the mixer. Different cast and hit families can overlap. The Effects slider and master mute control playback; Music and Ambience retain their existing settings and crossfades.

Game sends full ability IDs on successful casts and accepted damage. Existing damage, range, resources, cooldowns, rewards and invulnerability checks still decide whether combat succeeds. Spell reaction signals continue to drive the existing magical ambience. Audio does not predict unsupported public combat; the offline combat guards remain in place.

Run `./godot.sh --headless --script tests/test_combat_audio.gd` for real stream decoding, provenance hashes, one-shot duration/loop flags, catalogue rejection, variant cycling, hit burst suppression and mute. `tests/test_combat_audio_game.gd` checks all twenty-four real casts, rejected casts, homing contact, explosion, hurt/guard feedback, invulnerability, combat RNG and online refusals. Both are in the ordinary checker.

Run `./godot.sh --script tools/check_combat_audio.gd` with the native audio driver to check each profile reaching the master mixer, clipping, voice bounds, actual mute and effects-volume amplitude. It writes `artifacts/combat-audio-preview.wav` and an indexed `combat-audio-preview.json`. Native mixer acceptance establishes playback and routing; final sound balance still benefits from speaker and headphone playtesting. `tools/check_audio.gd` separately checks the existing music playback and crossfade behavior.

The WAVs use normal Godot audio imports so exported resource loading follows the existing packaging path. Their first preparation used an isolated audio-only project; the main editor/importer, deployed builds and public manifest were not changed. Future full-source release snapshots must include these audio bytes and import metadata before packaging.

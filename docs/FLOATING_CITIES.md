# Lanternwake and floating cities

Lanternwake is the first floating city and the first playable destination for a new solo character or a fresh public-world account. Four suspended districts connect through sky bridges. The Commons receive travelers, the Moonlit Guild Walk holds guild halls, the Ember Market has trading counters, and the Worldgate Dock opens a route to the wandering lands.

Its first seasonal appearance is the Lantern Vigil, inspired by Halloween. Violet stone, arcane crystals, pumpkin lanterns and candles share one fixed layout. The arrival ring and bridge approaches stay clear. The city has no hostile creature spawns, and collision prevents walking into the surrounding sky.

Press E at the arrival board to read the city introduction. Solo travelers can open the public-world sign-in from that board. Public sessions show the actual nearby travelers received from the server. This is a shared meeting lobby. Party formation, matchmaking and guild membership require later server services.

The Lantern Wardens, Moonlit Archive and Coppermoon Artificers have readable guild charters. Solo characters can enter their halls through the charter panel, using the existing inn, shrine and workshop room types. The two Ember Market counters use the existing finite shop stock, coin purses and owned-item transactions. Online market transactions and hall instances remain gated until their server handlers exist.

Press E beside the southern Worldgate to depart. The matching arrival ring near the natural world's starting city returns to Lanternwake. New solo journeys also have Return to Lanternwake in the pause menu while on the surface; recall uses the existing safe-ground checks. Saving preserves the city, season, inventory and shop stock, including Sanctum and indoor return paths. Existing solo saves keep their recorded destination and generation edition. Existing online accounts keep their stored positions.

Public gate travel sends a destination choice rather than coordinates. The server checks the player's actual gate distance, chooses the landing and saves it. Off-gate and malformed requests leave the session alive. The client waits for the complete destination chunk/region window, then snaps the player and camera to the server position. Returning replays the pruned map records. No client terrain generation is used for the hub or its destination.

`config/floating_city.json` separates permanent islands, bridges, hall/store IDs, approaches and collision from seasonal colors and ornaments. `halloween` is the default; `arcane` provides an alternate appearance for validation. `FloatingHub.set_theme()` changes the solo appearance without moving facilities or changing stock IDs. Future seasonal profiles can use these same decoration slots. Automatic calendar changes and server season administration are future work.

`floating_city.gd` owns the fixed floor and facility records. `hub_world.gd` adds the city to the ordinary World interface while preserving natural terrain and editions4/5/6. `floating_city_view.gd` renders the received island layout, existing licensed Halloween assets, halls and counters. `floating_hub.gd` handles arrival boards, solo travel and seasonal state. The optional `progress.floating_city` save field selects the compatible world adapter during reconstruction. Public chunks use the existing exact map codec and authority manifest.

`tests/test_floating_city.gd` checks connected service approaches, sky collision, fixed seasonal identities, unchanged natural terrain and exact remote records for editions4/6. `tests/test_floating_city_game.gd` checks the actual edition6 arrival, charter, purchase, disk/home return, travel/recall and malformed seasonal state. Both belong to the ordinary checker. `server/test/floating_city.test.mjs` starts an isolated real gateway and checks authenticated co-spawn, movement, gate refusals, travel, interest filtering and restart persistence.

`tests/test_floating_city_online.gd` needs an external isolated gateway and matching revision. It checks two actual Game accounts, peer visibility, Commons, gate movement, destination streaming/camera alignment, return and token resume. It is excluded from the ordinary checker because it requires that server. `--capture` writes four native online images. `./godot.sh --script tools/capture_floating_city.gd` writes six offline arrival, overview, charter, purchase and dock images. These fixtures isolate saves and settings. Native rendering and focused network tests pass; browser release, hosting and combined project acceptance remain with their publication owners.

# Roads, patrols and creature grounds

Road merchants, couriers, pilgrims, scholars and wilderness scouts travel between neighboring towns. They follow the generated highways, stop at town approaches, rest, and turn back. Two town guards patrol the street cross and its approaches. Travelers stop and face the player at conversation distance. Press E to speak to them; their route names come from the actual linked towns.

Traveling merchants exchange ember shards for vitality potions, recovery potions and rune lanterns. Every second creature kill drops a shard alongside its existing varied loot; collect the physical pickup to spend it. The dialogue lists each price and your shard balance. A failed purchase leaves inventory unchanged, including when the backpack is full. Offers are repeatable and configured in `config/world_activity.json`; the existing inventory and journey save retain purchased goods. This feature does not implement finite merchant stock or a separate currency.

Town activity alternates watch changes and road departures, markets and gear repairs, storytelling circles, pilgrim rests and courier exchanges. The original fountain supper and local delivery retain their times and factual event IDs. Every additional episode exposes the same active/aftermath contract and leaves a short visible trace. Regional-history metadata can add notes for these event kinds. Markets and story circles put goods or books on a table; scenes use the existing clear plaza anchor and add no terrain obstacles.

Monsters spawn in eight wilderness grounds per region, plus the outskirts of each actual ruin. The original den, hunting ground and first ruin retain their IDs. Six added clearings, haunts, ranges, hollows, roaming grounds and refuges use seeded positions and varied radii. Names adapt to forest, meadow, desert and frost contexts. Each area owns a stable ID, center and radius. A seeded group shares an eligible species from the existing biome/facet pools; cells across a habitat boundary can select another locally eligible species. Members start at least two cells apart on land inside the area. Roads, bridges, town streets, water and settlement clearances are excluded. Each chunk still contains at most two planned combatants. Some terrain cannot support a full group, so quiet areas remain possible. Ground membership also resolves across region edges.

Surface packs keep personal space while idle, chasing, attacking or returning. Space follows each model's width; even creatures at exactly the same point choose opposing directions and spread apart. Steering stays within the existing movement speed, terrain collision, town protection and ground leash. Monsters chase within their grounds plus a small margin, then stop attacking and return toward their individual spawn positions. Guards deter creatures that approach within 3.5 cells. Deterrence does not damage enemies or award kills, loot or dungeon progress. The minimap shows nearby grounds as muted rings, merchants as gold dots, guards as blue dots and other peaceful NPCs as pale dots. The location label identifies an occupied ground.

## Runtime and saved state

`WorldLife` retains up to twelve local residents/animals, eight road travelers/guards and four [peaceful wilderness walkers](WILDERNESS_NPCS.md), with 24 actor models total. The added walkers include herbalists, cartographers, prospectors, storytellers, wanderers and trail couriers. Existing local routing remains limited to one query per step and 169 searched cells. Road actors move along known street/highway segments without searching the whole world. Nearby routes are generated from the current region and its neighbors; unloaded traveler positions derive from the saved world clock. Towns, fauna media, terrain and asset definitions remain owned by their existing catalogues.

Road IDs are `road:x:y:axis:slot`; town-watch IDs are `watch:x:y:slot`. Saved records include point, current short target, heading, activity, next-decision tick, waypoint, direction and rest-until tick. Validation rejects malformed IDs, positions outside the owning road/town envelope, nonfinite values and invalid waypoints. Restoring the active set preserves its saved positions before subsequent streaming populates new groups. Saves without life progress retain the existing deterministic defaults. This activity feature adds no geography reset.

`MonsterMotion` snapshots nearby combatant positions into two-cell buckets once per update. Spacing reads local neighbors rather than searching every combatant for every creature. Model widths are measured once at spawn. Pack steering and ground planning do not spend combat RNG. Dungeon movement and attack/reward rules retain their existing behavior.

The world clock advances in unpaused overworld and dungeon play. Dialogue, readers, menus, death, interiors and the Sanctum keep their existing pause behavior. Travelers and guards stay in the peaceful actor scheduler; combatants, gate counts, reward lists and combat RNG remain separate.

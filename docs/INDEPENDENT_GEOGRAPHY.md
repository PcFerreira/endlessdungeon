# Independent geography

Edition 5 introduced independent geography. New offline characters now use edition 6, which retains that planning and adds [larger biome regions](BIOMES.md). Low-level generation and debug calls keep their edition-4 defaults. A saved journey restores its own edition; initializing an edition preserves existing slot, backup and temporary files.

`SitePlanner` selects towns, independent surface places and dungeons through separate seeded decisions. Planning cells can contain no town or no site. Town centers vary within their cells. A guaranteed real town in each four-by-four district connects the settlement network; additional towns and surface destinations connect to actual towns. The starter retains a safe town and two introductory dungeon candidates. Placement reserves footprints, clearance and entry corridors.

`World.generate(seed, 5)` materializes those plans. Sites carry their actual cell, facing, approach, footprint, biome and height. `region.city` is empty when there is no town. `World.sites_in`, `site_by_id`, `city_at` and `nearest_site` query actual generated records. Far settlement lookup can resolve the district's real anchor without inventing a city. Dungeon families use their actual entrance climate; entry and surface return follow the generated approach.

Road records identify canonical endpoints and paths. Their common height field keeps crossings consistent, and sharp outside-town bends include full deck coverage. The actual raster centerline is recorded separately from bridge railing and shoulder metadata. Town turns use reserved centerlines to avoid fixed buildings. The middle of each route avoids both endpoint footprints before joining their entrance stubs. This prevents a route from crossing a destination's back wall. These choices retain the original town house, door, use and ownership IDs.

Independent ruins, castles, churches and temples have structures, rotated collision and purposeful fixture groups defined in `config/surface_locations.json`. Ruins have broken archive walls and a work area. Castles have a gated enclosure, keep, towers and courtyard supplies. Churches have a chapel, graves and a forecourt shelter. Temples have columns, side canopies, an altar and offerings. Castle and church bodies use the existing architecture templates; other structures and fixtures use their actual cultural roles. Every body follows its owning site's geometric family, with source or derivative identities retained.

Each place has a fixed lectern and an existing readable book, a physically accessible reading approach, and a rest point. Rest restores health and mana only when the normal danger gate permits it; it preserves the player's town sanctuary. Roads connect the real entrance to the reader and shelter. Actual supported portable goods have explicit empty ownership. Their rendered support chain, access cells, pickup order, streamed source masks and disk state use the existing custody system. Readers and structural parts remain fixed. The location name appears in the area HUD; only Book and Shelter / Rest labels appear above their uses.

Edition-5 roadside clues select actual clear ground beside their region's connected road records. They name a real nearby town and dungeon; neither has to share the marker's planning cell. Completely site-free regions receive no discovery marker. Town histories keep actual resident and ledger origins, and can refer to an actual neighboring dungeon when their own cell has none. Town-free clues do not invent town archives or witnesses. Quest offers reuse that exact history target and resolve its real family book, floor count and cell. Saved edition-5 targets must match the seed's actual planner records. Collected text remains preserved through content revisions, and marking a route reports only that exact site to the atlas.

Roadside marker models retain the authored source ID and use the existing source-preserving family adapter. Their longest ground dimension and height follow the clue definition while their horizontal proportions remain those of the rendered source. The saved clue does not gain an inventory item or ownership record.

Edition-5 travelers follow the real settlement paths, pause at endpoints and reverse through their waypoints. Life snapshots carry an optional seed/edition context. Validation reconstructs actual planner towns and roads, checks positions against them and rejects invented routes or mismatched contexts. Legacy edition-4 life records retain their original centers and cardinal routes. Missing optional life state initializes the restored edition, including inside the Sanctum.

## Evidence and remaining work

The pure planner checks 324 seeded records, footprint clearance, independent counts, canonical paths and bounded negative/far replay. The staged World check covers 50 materialized regions, their declared raster paths and adjacent centerline grades, plus physical traversal through streamed chunks. The traveler check follows two complete curved itineraries through actual movement and chunk streaming, verifies endpoint reversal and reconstructs JSON saves. The actual Game check covers jittered-town fresh-scene saves, town-free rendering/audio/stores/sanctuary behavior, physical dungeon E, oriented return and nested home saves. Legacy World, life, activity, disk saves, ambience and finite stores have separate regressions.

`tools/capture_independent_world.gd` produces six actual Game views in `artifacts/independent-world`. These scripted captures verify the selected views and interactions. They do not constitute an ordinary fifteen-minute expedition or prove visibility from every camera angle.

Surface-place checks cover 23 actual generated places, all four purposes and facings, streamed entrance-to-reader/rest access and exact collision. A separate 64-composition matrix checks all four climates and facings against actual rendered dimensions, source/derivative identities and support contact. Actual Game acceptance reads and rests at all four purposes, refuses healing near enemies, takes real unowned goods in support order and restores their masks, knowledge and home return in a fresh scene. Legacy surface custody and edition-4 disk restoration pass separately.

`tools/capture_surface_locations.gd` produces 32 actual Game views: each purpose in every generated climate from opposing cameras at surface zoom 24. `artifacts/surface-locations/sites.json` records the real site identities, cells and facings. All views were inspected after removing an overlapping site-name label. These are scripted composition captures, not expedition evidence. Native review still shows the existing bright source roofs and substantial paved site footprints; detailed material variety and ordinary camera/pacing acceptance are separate work.

Sparse target checks cover 50 regions: 30 roadside clues, including 17 town-free clues, ten completely empty quiet regions, nine town histories with neighboring targets and fifteen accepted/restored quest records. They verify physical marker access after streaming, exact target rejection and completable sources. The marker matrix checks all four authored purposes in both geometry families, actual source identity, grounding and horizontal proportions. Actual Game acceptance reads a town-free clue, marks its destination, accepts a neighboring-dungeon quest, reads the actual ledger, enters its real dungeon through E, inspects the accessible archive and returns for a one-time reward. Fresh-scene disk/home restoration and repeat-reward refusal pass. Separate legacy discovery/history/quest Game checks retain edition-4 behavior.

`tools/capture_sparse_regional.gd` records five views: opposing actual town-free clue views, its physical E reader, a town ledger and the collected history's journal reader naming a neighboring dungeon. They are in `artifacts/sparse-regional`; the companion `targets.json` contains their generated identities. These are scripted interaction views.

Edition-6 fungal and cinder ecology profiles bind their eligible dungeon and regional facet contexts to the actual generated climate. Edition 5 keeps its original staged facet behavior for saved worlds. A possible civic/player occlusion in the jittered-town view still needs ordinary camera review. Current biome-family work and the newer explicit generated-voxel exceptions for NPCs and dungeon monsters remain coordinated in `chat.md`. Full integration and ordinary expedition review remain broader gates. Public browser/server work has separate owners and acceptance, with persistent worlds retaining their stored edition.

# Stores and markets

Press E at a counter in a generated shop or workshop, or beside an actual outdoor market stall. The Trade label and interaction hint identify the approach. Indoor exits, rest points and books keep their existing interaction priority. Nearby watch demands and outdoor building entrances also keep priority. Esc closes trading.

Goods appear as inventory-style icon tiles. Trader stock and Your goods switch the displayed collection; Previous and Next page through up to 24 goods at a time. Select a tile to inspect stock, ownership and prices in the detail pane, then use Buy or Sell. Traveling merchants show offer cards with their coin/shard payment actions. See [ICON_GRID_UI.md](ICON_GRID_UI.md) for the shared collection layout.

Buy with Gold Coins, or sell eligible goods from your backpack. The trader rejects stolen quantities and preserves an equipped copy. A full backpack or overflowing coin stack cancels the whole transaction, including payment and stock. Traveling merchants retain their existing separate coin/shard barter.

Each real building or market lot has its own finite stock and coin purse. Buying reduces stock and funds the purse. Selling adds stock and pays from that purse. Stock does not refresh by closing a window, leaving town, visiting the Sanctum or loading a save. There is no timed restocking or merchant schedule in this slice. Sell prices are below buy prices. Initial prices are tuning values.

`config/stores.json` defines specialties, building-use and interior/lot mappings, counter approaches, item-kind/slot filters, staple goods, prices, rarity multipliers, stock and buyback rates. `scripts/store_catalog.gd` queries the current item definitions, including dynamically imported bundles. No runtime pack list or future item list is required. Retired items, currencies, bound objects and generic world props are excluded. New items with existing eligible kinds/slots enter stock pools. New kinds and specialist semantics need corresponding catalogue metadata and filter support.

Five specialties currently cover provisions, expedition equipment, workshop goods, charms/lamps and market wares. The actual `use_id` selects a specialty; interior-type defaults cover older houses. Every current shop/workshop layout has a connected counter approach. Outdoor trading uses the existing market lot ID and a walkable edge beside its reserved counter. It adds no new NPC, model, collision obstacle or source object. Existing rendered wares retain their construction/ownership records and do not imply that every displayed catalogue good sits physically on the counter. New item models appear in the world only through the existing deliberate carrying/placement flow; regional construction and NPC family selection remain under their own catalogues.

Stock uses actual generated building/lot IDs, with seed-derived initial choices independent of combat RNG. `progress.stores` is optional and defaults to an empty ledger in older saves. Saved entries retain their resolved specialty, stock and purse when reopened. JSON quantities are validated and normalized to integers. Generator version 4 and save envelope version 2 are unchanged. The ledger holds up to 4,096 visited traders and 128 product types per trader; new traders refuse when the ledger is full. Stock is capped at 99 units per product. Transactions refuse before exceeding purse/save bounds.

The acceptance test uses real Game shop entry and market trading, all four current business layouts, finite stock exhaustion, empty/full purse refusal, equipped/stolen goods and stolen coins, both directions of backpack rollback, Sanctum carry, source identity, fresh disk restore and older/malformed saves. `tools/capture_stores.gd` captures actual shop/workshop/market approaches, stock windows and successful coin purchases. Human economy balance, long-session ledger growth and specialized town-theme stock remain separate follow-up work.

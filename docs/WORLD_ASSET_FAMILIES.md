# Biome asset families

The later explicit character replacement requests use [generated voxel NPCs](VOXEL_CHARACTERS.md) in every climate and [generated voxel monsters](VOXEL_MONSTERS.md) inside dungeons. Their actual geometry is block_voxel, with biome-specific clothing or monster accent palettes. This supersedes the imported NPC cohort and dungeon-enemy body requirements below; imported source classification remains available for surface hostiles, fauna and retained assets. Other world-asset family rules still apply.

The user requires every generated local asset to follow the owning biome's visual family. This includes terrain and architecture, civic forms, vegetation, cultural props, crops, residents and watch, local fauna and enemies, generated rewards, and dungeon surfaces and contents. A coherent source series within a town must also belong to the biome's family.

## Current contract

`config/environment_styles.json` is the shared biome-to-family definition. `scripts/environment_styles.gd` exposes `family_for(biome)`, `family_of(asset_id)` and `allows(asset_id, biome)`. The current mapping is:

| Biome | Visual family | Geometry |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0, 1 | `block_voxel` | Block-built voxel forms |
| 2, 3 | `flat_lowpoly` | Faceted flat-color forms |

`allows` accepts only a classified model or valid native role belonging to the required family. Unknown assets, invalid native IDs and unknown biomes are rejected. Registry labels such as `fantasy` or `lowpoly` are not compatibility evidence. Model queries identify reviewed source series; new queries must follow visual review of actual models. Optional `source_catalogues` import reviewed series queries with explicit `environment_family` metadata; the current sources are the six-series NPC catalogue, Aster's architecture catalogue, the creature catalogue and the item catalogue. Ambiguous classification or an unknown series family invalidates the classifier.

`derived_catalogues` registers known template/family combinations, currently `town_architecture:<family>:<template>`. Original building sources remain faceted models. Block towns require actual voxel conversion before displaying a block derivative. The declared derived identity makes the shared gate usable; it does not prove that conversion geometry, source color, openings or bounds are correct. Those require the architecture renderer's real-model acceptance.

`native_dungeon_roles` registers generated floors, high/low walls, stairs, portals, reward chests and gate motes as `native_dungeon:<family>:<role>`. Unknown roles stay unclassified. `DungeonSurfaces` produces block masonry or bevelled faceted surfaces while retaining the dungeon's separate stone palette. The faceted mesh winding follows [Godot's SurfaceTool front-face convention](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_surfacetool.html), with a triangle/normal regression and native verification.

Selection filters by family before building a regional palette. A missing compatible role or unavailable resource uses grounded procedural geometry of that same family. The fallback carries its actual native ID and `visual_family`, rather than retaining the unavailable imported model's identity. Metadata must describe rendered geometry. Supported native roles are exposed by `supports(role)`; `native_model(role, family, footprint, height)` fits exact grounded dimensions.

## Context and source identity

Wilderness uses the actual cell biome. Town construction and local residents/watch use the actual city's biome. Dungeons and independent sites need their owning site's biome; a dungeon's material palette index is not a biome. Dungeon-family eligibility, item ecology and town source-series coherence remain additional filters.

Keep stable city, site, house, lot and source-part IDs while changing selection. Resolved assembly descriptors retain the actual model ID, family, support and bounds. Family matching does not replace connectivity, purposeful density, ground placement, support or ownership validation.

The user's earlier instruction permits broader NPC appearances on roads/open spaces. Those actors are visitors; generated town residents and watch use the local family. Existing carried or deliberately placed objects retain their recorded item/source identity while their world presentation adapts to the destination family. Inventory quantities, custody, source masks, support links and saved records remain authoritative. `ITEM_FAMILIES.md` records the tested derivative and fitting policy.

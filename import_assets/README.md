# Source asset staging

The [downloaded asset catalog](free_packs/README.md) lists 50 free packs with voxel and low poly models, isometric pixel art and sprites, inventory icons, music, sound effects, Christmas/Halloween props, and medieval fantasy HUD assets. Original publisher license files are preserved. Each pack also has source records, the applicable license text and file hashes.

Use [ATTRIBUTION.md](free_packs/ATTRIBUTION.md) for the six CC BY packs that require credits (five CC BY 3.0 and one CC BY 4.0). Forty-four packs use CC0. The earlier packs and music elsewhere in this folder retain their existing licenses and attribution; this catalog does not change those terms.

The [HUD shortlist](free_packs/HUD_ASSETS.md) maps medieval magic HUD elements to the downloaded frames, resource bars, pixel controls and spell icons.

One additional forest pack is in [license_review](license_review/README.md) because its publisher license statements conflict. Its files and original notices are preserved.

The folder's `.gdignore` keeps these source collections out of the game's normal Godot import scan. Selected assets can be copied into `assets/` with their dependencies, licenses and credits when adopted.

The [item variation guide](free_packs/ITEM_VARIETY.md) maps the new equipment, food, crafting, gem and potion packs to an endless-world loot plan.

The [world expansion guide](free_packs/WORLD_ASSETS.md) lists biome scenery, caves, camping, interiors, town modules, workshop props, NPC outfits, monsters, arcane effects and ambient recordings.

The [architecture guide](free_packs/ARCHITECTURE_ASSETS.md) lists castle modules, medieval settlement buildings, village construction pieces and a temple/palace kit.
